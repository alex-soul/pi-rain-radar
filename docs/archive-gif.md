# Export Archive playback as a GIF

Load an Archive window, arrange the screen, and let playback load smoothly. Open the selected date/window, then **Other → Export GIF**. The Archive **Window** tab holds the date, time, playback window and Load button; **Options** holds playback speed, final-frame hold, comparison and provider display. The preview shows the current frame; generation always includes one complete selected window, oldest to newest, at the selected Archive speed and final-frame hold.

Choose **Current view**, **Main map only**, or **Overview map only**. Current view includes visible widgets, charts and docks. Map-only views isolate that map without overlapping widgets; their existing layers, opacity, geography and supported decorations remain. Overview can be exported while its widget is closed. Main-map exports use the current viewport crop and include its enabled distance scale. Overview uses its configured aspect ratio and displayed size (390 × 280 before its first opening). Neither mode changes zoom.

Output size scales proportionally. Colour detail selects 64, 128 or 256 colours per frame; it does not skip frames or change speed. Loop repeats the cycle; switch it off to play once. Current view has separate temporary options to hide buttons and handles. Hide handles removes dock outlines and LEDs as well as widget resize/drag handles. To leave a dock out, tuck it away on screen before opening export; exports inherit the current dock state. Required credits remain visible and scale with the output image. Help is available through the standard `(i)` buttons.

Generation and download run in the browser on the device being used. The appliance serves history/media, including any assets not still in the browser cache. No server-side renderer, upload or external capture service is involved. Keep the tab open and its viewport unchanged. Preparation verifies each frame’s selected assets, including visible camera snapshots, immediately before rendering it. Retained metadata gaps stay gaps; an unavailable image produces an explicit error, never a substituted frame. Hidden or unselected widgets do not become export dependencies.

Playback is held while previewing/exporting. Historical readings and widgets follow each captured frame. Current view includes the Archive actual-rainfall switch state, original forecast bars, observed midpoint dots and smooth connecting line, with the labelled mm/h Y axis and no heading; map-only exports exclude this widget. The current clock and operational statistics stay at their opening values; they are not historical telemetry. Success, cancellation and failure restore the previous cursor and playing/paused state. Download the result before closing the popup; closing releases it. Saved screen settings are unchanged. The Archive settings panel remembers its moved position in this browser across closing and refreshing, and stays within the viewport when resized.

GIF uses a limited palette and delays rounded to 10 milliseconds. Photos, gradients and browser backdrop effects may differ from the original view. Review the generated animation before sharing. The preview before generation shows the composition before colour reduction.

The exporter processes one frame at a time and uses one encoding worker. The output limit is 8 megapixels per frame. Source assets are retained only for the current transition and static composition, with a 128 MB working-set limit rather than a whole-window limit. Encoded frames are transferred into browser-managed Blob chunks; the worker does not retain the full GIF, and there is no fixed 128 MB output cap. Full 24-hour windows at 1280 × 720 retain all frames and the selected quality. Available browser memory/storage and rendering time still depend on the device and content. It stops with an explanation rather than silently reducing a window or its quality. The asset store is released after generation; the worker is terminated on completion or cancellation. Actual browser memory also includes decoded layers, the DOM capture and temporary encoding buffers. A smaller output or shorter window reduces work. Laptop validation does not establish Pi performance; Pi acceptance belongs to the RC hardware check.

Capture uses locally bundled [html-to-image](https://github.com/bubkoo/html-to-image) and encoding uses [gifenc](https://github.com/mattdesl/gifenc). Their MIT licences and the small capture adaptations are recorded in `public/vendor/`.

## Storm showcase and known limitation

The [Austen and Boelo gallery](gallery.md#storm-replays) contains two full-day, 1280 × 720 exports at 8× on repeat. Those storms inspired this feature.

In 0.14.0, a Current view export can show the playback slider thumb without its background track. This is an accepted cosmetic limitation; frame selection and playback still work. A fix is deferred to a future release.
